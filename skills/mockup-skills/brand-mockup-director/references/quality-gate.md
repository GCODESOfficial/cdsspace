# Photoreal Mockup Quality Gate

## A. Artwork fidelity

- All supplied text matches exactly.
- Logo geometry, spacing, symbols, and orientation are unchanged.
- Brand colors remain consistent with the source artwork.
- No invented copy, icons, barcodes, QR codes, legal text, or decorative marks.
- Artwork is not mirrored, stretched, squeezed, or cropped unintentionally.
- Panel-specific artwork appears on the correct panel.

## B. Geometry

- Object proportions look physically manufacturable.
- Board, plastic, glass, metal, or fabric thickness is visible where expected.
- Folds, seams, gussets, lids, caps, handles, closures, and openings make sense.
- Rounded edges have small realistic bevels rather than mathematically sharp edges.
- Cylinders remain circular in perspective and do not collapse into ovals incorrectly.

## C. Material and print

- Roughness, reflection, transparency, and texture match the named substrate.
- Foil behaves like metal, not yellow ink.
- Spot UV appears only in its mask and reflects the environment.
- Emboss or deboss affects geometry, shadow, and highlight.
- Printed artwork inherits surface grain, folds, and light.
- Ink density and black levels are believable.

## D. Lighting and photography

- The product has a grounded contact shadow.
- Reflections correspond to actual lights or environment sources.
- Highlight size matches light size.
- No edge halo, cutout fringe, or inconsistent color temperature.
- Depth of field does not blur the brand's key information.
- Perspective and focal length suit the product category.

## E. Composition

- Main panel is readable and visually dominant.
- Product is not cropped accidentally.
- Negative space is intentional.
- Background supports the brand and does not compete with it.
- Supporting objects are scale-consistent and contextually relevant.

## F. Technical output

- Review at 100 percent zoom.
- Export in sRGB for normal digital delivery unless another color space is requested.
- Keep a layered or editable master when the workflow supports it.
- Preserve transparent background as a separate output when useful.
- Avoid destructive resizing before the final export.
- Keep the longest edge at least 2000 px for presentation use unless the user requests a smaller size.
